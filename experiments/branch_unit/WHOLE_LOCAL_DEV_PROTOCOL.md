# Whole-local hierarchical branch development protocol

Status: `frozen_for_seeded_N7_edge_envelope_v4_development`

This protocol supersedes the current pure-L development attempt.  The pure-L
attempt is retained as a failed experiment because its L1 implementation did
not consume sibling branches and therefore did not test joint BranchUnit
generation.

## Development question

Can one complete repeat be generated coherently when a global first-level
branch plan and local child-branch generation are executed together?

This is a representation-feasibility experiment, not an L-only causal
comparison and not paper evidence.  L/C/G ablations remain blocked until the
complete structure passes the visual gate.

## Input boundary

The generator may consume only:

1. the sampled backbone and its tangents;
2. the two flower positions and reserve ellipses;
3. the repeat x-range and canvas bounds;
4. the prototype id.

Every first-, second-, and third-level branch is generated by this experiment.
No SVG branch, guide, inherited attachment, growth region, space sample, region
graph, or branch-derived clearance field may be read.  The existing ignored-
field intervention proof must leave the stripped input and output unchanged.

## Staged topology

The first gate contains exactly:

- 7 first-level branches attached directly to the backbone;
- 2 of the 7 first-level branches terminate at the two flower collars;
- 5 free first-level branches;
- 4 second-level branches, each attached to a different non-flower first-level
  parent;
- 2 third-level branches attached to registered second-level parents;
- no deeper branch.

The 8-first-level variant is not generated until the 7-first-level result
passes the visual gate.  Its child budget will be frozen separately rather than
silently copied.

## Global-local generation contract

The global plan is one closed-form seeded mapping for the complete repeat.  It
fixes the role corridors, flower assignments, and child-count budgets, then
derives the actual mount fractions, lengths, directions, arm ratios, bend mode,
and child parameters from the seed before any curve is compiled.  Parameters
are therefore replayable but are not constant from one generation to another.

The three pre-registered development seeds are `4101`, `4102`, and `4103`.
They are all generated and retained; no result may be selected away.  Different
seeds must have different plan digests and geometry hashes.  Each seed is also
built twice: raw plan/geometry object equality, canonical structural JSON bytes,
and geometry hashes must all match.  Raster-file byte reproducibility is not
claimed by this structural-core proof.

The strict source canvas is `1024 x 304`, and the visible development crop is
exactly the first repeat `x=[0,256]`, hence `256 x 304` structural units.  The
canvas, repeat origin/range, and height are all checked; shifted or differently
sized inputs are rejected.  NaN/infinity, non-positive flower radii, and
invalid backbone fractions are also rejected before planning.  Three
composition relations are part of the global plan:

- the `flower_1` support root is restricted to the backbone trough around
  `s=0.40` with at most `0.01` seed variation.  Global rhythm interventions do
  not move this registered trough root, and the validator checks the band;
- the central balance branch grows from around `s=0.62`, but does not touch the
  literal top boundary.  Its tip must be 8--16 structural units higher than the
  tip of the right upper branch generated by the same seed;
- one shared visible inset envelope is generated directly from global seed
  variables before any per-branch parameter is derived or any branch is
  compiled.  No local tip may be used to infer that envelope.
  `primary_2_free`, `primary_3_free`, and `primary_6_balance` are registered to
  its left, bottom, and top sides respectively.  Their centreline endpoints are
  inset by half the stroke width, so their complete visible strokes reach but
  never cross the same envelope.  `primary_7_free` is registered as a top-right
  internal stop: it remains 8--16 units inside both registered approach sides,
  and its top path may not overshoot the tip and return.  `secondary_3a`, whose
  tip enters the bottom approach band, is
  explicitly registered as an internal bottom stop 4--8 units above the
  visible envelope and likewise may not overshoot before ending.
  `secondary_2a` is registered to approach the left band and then turn inward;
  `tertiary_3a_1` is registered to approach the bottom band and finish parallel
  to or inward from it.  Both must increase their clearance after the closest
  point.  Every other branch must remain inside the envelope.

An envelope-target branch must leave with a tangent parallel to its registered
side.  Its terminal handle must occupy at least 6% of the whole curve length,
so the change of direction begins before the final few pixels.  An internal
branch may stop earlier; it is not stretched merely to fill the envelope.  No
branch receives an independent farther boundary, and clipping cannot be used
to hide a protrusion.

The visible-stroke model is frozen as a centered stroke with `round` linecaps
and `round` linejoins.  Exact cubic derivative roots, not sampled points, define
the centreline extrema; half the stroke width is then added to obtain the
visible extrema.  The whole curve, not only its tip, is checked against a
side-specific approach band: 4% of repeat width for left/right and 4% of repeat
height for top/bottom.  Exact cubic/threshold crossings partition each curve
into in-band intervals.  An initial root interval may be ignored only while the
curve leaves that side inward; after it exits, every later interval or even a
tangential band contact is checked again.  Every other band entry must be
registered for that exact side; registering one side never authorizes contact
with another.  An unauthorized band entry or visible-stroke contact is a hard
failure at every hierarchy level.

Validation recomputes the global envelope from the strict P0 and seed.  It does
not trust the mutable diagnostic copy stored in the plan; any disagreement
between the declaration and the recomputed values is itself a hard failure.

This N7 image is evaluated as one visible repeat crop.  Whether a horizontal
side later becomes a periodic wrap seam is a separate tiling experiment and is
not inferred from the present edge treatment.

Each local BranchUnit then consumes only its registered parent curve and its
predeclared child rows.  It may derive a child root and tangent from that parent
but may not inspect already rendered siblings, validation diagnostics, or an
occupancy map.  There is one compile pass followed by one read-only validation
pass.

For this development gate there is:

- no candidate enumeration;
- no retry or resampling;
- no collision-driven repair;
- no beam search or backtracking;
- no deletion or replacement of failed branches.

All ranges are development parameters for `proto_sw_1_3`.  A failure is
retained and reported; changing a range creates a new numbered development run
and replays the same three seeds.

## Curve and bend contract

Every semantic branch is audited independently, including second- and
third-level branches.

- A first-level flower branch uses at most two C1-connected, degree-elevated
  quadratic Bezier segments.
- A free first-level branch uses one or two segments from the same compiler
  family.
- Every level uses at most two C1-connected, degree-elevated quadratic
  segments.  Each non-degenerate quadratic has one constant curvature sign, so
  every semantic branch has at most two bends by construction.
- The sampled tangent-lobe audit is a redundant measurement and must report
  `bend_count <= 2` for every curve.  Segment count alone is not accepted as
  the measurement.

## Hard structural gate

The N7 result fails if any condition is false:

1. topology counts and parent ids match the frozen plan;
2. every root lies on its registered parent; its opening direction is a closed-
   form 24--52 degree rotation from the parent tangent toward the branch travel
   direction, and never comes from collision feedback;
3. both flower branches end on the correct collar and do not enter the flower
   reserve early;
4. non-target branches do not enter a flower reserve;
5. every curve remains inside both the repeat/canvas bounds and the shared
   stroke-aware visible envelope;
6. every curve has at most two effective bends and no self-intersection;
7. after the allowed root contact zone, a branch does not touch its parent or
   the backbone again;
8. unrelated branches do not intersect and satisfy stroke-aware clearance;
9. input stripping and ignored-field intervention tests pass;
10. replay from the same seed has equal raw structural objects, canonical JSON
    bytes, and geometry hashes, while all three seeds produce distinct plans
    and geometry;
11. the trough flower root and central/right height relation satisfy the global
    composition constraints above;
12. exact-envelope, internal-stop, and turn-inward branches satisfy the rules
    for every side they enter; a registration for one side cannot excuse
    another; no unregistered curve enters a side band and no branch creates a
    visible-envelope overflow.

Validation is check-only.  A red result is not promoted by increasing a
clearance exemption after seeing the failure.

## Visual gate

Automatic validity is necessary but insufficient.  All three N7 images must
also show:

1. one readable whole-repeat rhythm rather than seven isolated sticks;
2. a clear first/second/third-level hierarchy;
3. natural flower support rather than a hook pasted onto an ellipse; flower
   collars are the intersections between each registered root-to-flower-center
   ray and the corresponding reserve ellipse;
4. child branches that grow from and continue the parent flow;
5. no snake-like curve, crowded knot, floating child, or obvious geometric
   placeholder;
6. useful occupied space without filling every gap uniformly;
7. first-level branches that read as long sweeps rather than short sticks, with
   visible but no more than two bends;
8. a central branch whose height is only slightly above the right branch, not a
   line stretched to the top border;
9. one coherent outer silhouette: edge-seeking branches flatten into the
   boundary direction or stop inside it, with no isolated tip protruding.

Only after the user accepts this image may the N8 gate begin.  Only after both
whole-local gates succeed may an L/C/G ablation protocol be frozen.
